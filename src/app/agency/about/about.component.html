WHO WE ARE

Certare

Certare is a technology consultancy focused on three practices: mobile development, web development and global testing. Senior engineers join your team, follow a disciplined process and back every release with evidence.

Mobile development

Apps for iOS and Android, tested on real devices, published to the stores without surprises.

Web development

Web platforms and APIs with automated end-to-end coverage wired into your pipeline.

Global testing

Manual, automation, API, performance and CI/CD quality gates across your whole stack.
